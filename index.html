📖 Tense Map

In S&T the tense changes for a few reasons. Learn the moves. The tag shows the level where each move first appears.

Move 1 · Reported speech: step one back in time Level 1–3

When the reporting verb is past (said, told, promised), the verb inside the quote moves one step back.

am / is / are"I am tired."
➜
was / wereShe said she was tired.
like / likes"My brother likes durians."
➜
liked…his brother liked durians.
am / is / are + -ing"I am waiting."
➜
was / were + -ing…she was waiting.
has / have + done"I have given…"
➜
had + done…she had given…
did (past simple)"I lost it yesterday."
➜
had + done…he had lost it the day before.
will · can · may · must"I will return it."
➜
would · could · might · had to…he would return it.

Time & place words move too Level 3

In the quoteReported
today / tonightthat day / that night
tomorrowthe next day / the following day
yesterdaythe day before / the previous day
next weekthe following week
now · here · thisthen · there · that

⚠️ Watch the people words: "I" said by Siti → "she". "My brother" said by Ravi → "his brother". "you" = the person being spoken to.

Move 2 · Reported questions: no more question order Level 3

Yes/No question"Are you hungry?" Mum asked me.
➜
asked + if / whether + person + verbMum asked me if I was hungry.
Wh- question"Where do you live?"
➜
asked + where + person + verb…asked Ravi where he lived.

Two checks: ① person BEFORE the verb (no "did he", "was he") ② the verb still steps back. "do / does / did" disappear.

Move 3 · Passive voice: keep the tense, add be Level 1–5

The thing that receives the action goes first. The tense lives in "be", and the main verb is always the 3rd form.

TenseActivePassive
presentThey cook it every day.It is cooked.
pastThe cat broke it.It was broken.
present perfectSomeone has stolen it.It has been stolen.
continuousThey are painting it.It is being painted.
past continuousThey were fixing it.It was being fixed.
will / mustThey will lock it.It will be locked.

One thing → is / was / has. More than one → are / were / have.

Move 4 · "since / for": from past till now Level 2

Started in the past and still true now → has / have + 3rd form (or has been + -ing).

Mr Tan started teaching here five years ago. → Mr Tan has taught at this school for five years. for + a length of time (five years) · since + a starting point (1980)

Move 5 · No sooner / Hardly / Scarcely: first event gets had Level 4

No sooner had + person + 3rd form … than …
Hardly / Scarcely had + person + 3rd form … when …

"had" jumps in front of the person: No sooner had Ethan reached… (not "Ethan had").

Move 6 · If only… / I wish… Level 4–5

A past that did not happen:

If + had + 3rd form, … would (not) have + 3rd form

Sam did not study. He failed. → If Sam had studied hard, he would not have failed. It DID happen? Then the "if" needs not: If we had not woken up late, …

wish steps the verb back too:

wish about NOW"I am not tall."
➜
wished he was / were tall"I don't have…" → wished he had…
regret about the PAST"I regret not bringing…"
➜
wished he had brought…regret eating → wished she had not eaten

Move 7 · Not until… did / Having + 3rd form Level 4–5

Jane did not leave until the rain stopped.
➜
Not until the rain stopped did Jane leave.did + person + base verb
Kai finished his homework. Then he went out.
➜
Having finished his homework, Kai went out.Having + 3rd form (no "had")

Move 8 · Word forms: same word, new job Level 1–5

Look at the word just before the blank. It tells you what kind of word you need.

a careful drivercareful = describes a person
➜

Siti decided to join.action word
➜
made a decisionafter a / the / his / great → a thing (noun)
full of dangera thing
➜
very dangerousafter very / was → a describing word

⚠️ Spelling traps: happy → happily · gentle → gently · explain → explanation · curious → curiosity

🔗 Joining Map

P3 Synthesis & Transformation: join two sentences into ONE, keep the meaning. The tag shows the level where each join first appears.

3 checks for every answer

  • ✅ Same meaning: nothing added, nothing lost (don't drop "a book", "later", "our").
  • ✅ Same tense as the question (was → was, likes → likes).
  • ✅ A name the first time, he / she / him after that.

Join 1 · Before / After Level 1

Number the actions first. Ask: "What happened first?"

Sarah packed her school bag. Then, she went to bed.
➜
Before going to bed, Sarah packed her school bag.After packing her school bag, Sarah went to bed.

Right after before / after → the -ing form: go → going · eat → eating.

⚠️ Spelling: swim → swimming · get → getting · come → coming · pack → packing (not "paking")

Join 2 · and / Both … and Level 1

Father is going. I am going too.
➜
Father and I are going to the market.two people → are / were / like (no s)
Mr Lim is taking lessons. Mr Tan is too.
➜
Both Mr Lim and Mr Tan are taking singing lessons.

Join 3 · because / so / as / since Level 1

because / as / since + the reason.   so + the result.

The homework was difficult. Daniel cried.
➜
Daniel cried because the homework was difficult.The homework was difficult, so Daniel cried.

Join 4 · Neither … nor / Either … or Level 2

Both are NO (does not, cannot)
➜
neither … norMegan likes neither carrots nor peas.
One of two is possible (can, may)
➜
either … orWe can play either badminton or table tennis.

⚠️ "Neither" is already the NO → no "not": Neither Manu nor Joshua could make it.

⚠️ The verb follows the person nearest to it: Neither Gemma nor her sister likes sandwiches.

Join 5 · but / yet / but not Level 2

Opposite or surprising → but / yet.

Ali is my friend. Sam is not my friend.
➜
Ali is my friend but not Sam.Short and neat — this is how your teacher marks it.

Join 6 · was …ing WHEN … Level 3

One action was already going on (was / were + -ing). Another action cut in.

ongoing action FIRST
➜
I was watching a movie when you called me.

⚠️ Not "You called me when I was watching a movie" — you flipped this 3 times (Wk 18, Wk 28).

Join 7 · Although Level 3

The bag was heavy, but Tim carried it home.
➜
Although the bag was heavy, Tim carried it home.

⚠️ Never although + but. Drop "However" and "Still," at the start. Remember the comma.

⚠️ John is named already? Then: the supporters cheered him and his teammates on.

Join 8 · prefer … TO … Level 4

Janice likes to sing. She likes to dance even more.
➜
Janice prefers dancing to singing.liked MORE → right after prefers · actions → -ing

⚠️ prefer … to, never "than" — you wrote "than" twice (Wk 36, Wk 40).

Join 9 · unless / until / Unlike Level 4

… if you do not add salt
➜
… unless you add saltunless = if … not → drop the "not"
We will wait … for the train to arrive.
➜
We will wait here until the next train arrives.after unless / until → present tense, no "will"
Desmond is different from his classmates.
➜
Unlike his classmates, Desmond likes to be by himself.

Join 10 · who / which / whose Level 5

WordForExample
whoa personThe pupils who ate contaminated food have fallen ill.
whicha thing / animalAmelia saw the teddy bear which was cute and fluffy.
whosebelongs toThis is Mrs Jones, whose son won the championship.

⚠️ who / which sits right after the word it describes: A shrill scream which came from the kitchen woke Ginger up.

Join 11 · Editing: fix the verb Level 1–5

A story that already happened
➜
past formgo → went · feel → felt · shake → shook · lose → lost
What always happens + he / she / it
➜
add sWhen Coco sits on a leaf, he turns green.

After did → base word (Did you eat?) · after was / were → -ing (were hunting)
